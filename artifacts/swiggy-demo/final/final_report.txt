KNOWN LIMITATIONS, RECORDED RATHER THAN HIDDEN
  1. Mappls is not used. The supplied Static Key is rejected by Mappls with
     "Client Credentials Expired ... client does not exist"; console
     email/password cannot authenticate the REST API. A fresh REST key
     (client_id + client_secret) from the Mappls console would be required.
     The basemap is instead rendered locally from the authentic OSM pilot
     extract, which also makes the recording independent of any tile server at
     run time. Nothing in the video claims Mappls.
  2. data/geo/bengaluru_clip.osm.pbf is byte-identical to
     andorra-latest.osm.pbf -- it is Andorra mislabelled as Bengaluru. It is
     NOT used anywhere in this demo and should be removed or re-cut.
  3. The mission's route reacts to live traffic, not to the later SIMULATED
     scenario: the scenario's +60% shock is applied to the frozen travel matrix
     the optimizer consumes, and the provider has no counterfactual route to
     return. Re-pricing the courier's ETA under the scenario would have meant
     inventing a number, so the mission is deliberately shown before the
     scenario rather than being restated after it.
  4. CP-SAT runs with allow_uncovered_demand=true, so it opens 4 facilities and
     the solver's own assignments cover 4 demand zones. The map therefore
     highlights the selected facilities rather than drawing assignment arcs;
     arcs would have implied coverage the solve did not produce.
  5. The local worker supplies the Pub/Sub transport that is absent off-cloud.
     The claim, lease, fencing, solve and persistence are the deployed code.
